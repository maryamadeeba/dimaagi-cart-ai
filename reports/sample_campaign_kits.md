# Win-back campaign kits

Model: `gemini-3.8-flash`. R refs = Yelp reviews of other grocery stores (proxy voice). T refs = published marketing and retail reports. Offers are suggestions for the owner to adjust. Remove every [CONFIRM] tag (and confirm the claim is true) before sending.

## Core · Basket changed

**Goal:** Win back 272 drifting Core regular shoppers who cut back on packaged bread, fluid milk, and cheese by reassuring them of in-store availability and freshness while providing a modest threshold incentive to rebuild basket size.

### Offer: Take $3 Off Your Next $20 Basket
Suggested values: $3 off your purchase of $20 or more at checkout. These values are suggestions and can be adjusted by the store.

*Perk:* none  
*Economics:* $3 off $20 (15% of basket); median weekly spend $9.65; threshold above median ✅; economics ✅

*Why this offer:* The segment currently spends a median of $9.65 per week. Setting a $20 minimum basket encourages shoppers to rebuild their baskets by picking up their usual dairy and bread staples, while a $3 discount meets the 15% maximum discount cap to protect thin grocery margins. (T1, T5, R1, R3)

### Before you send (owner checklist)
- [ ] Audit packaged bread and roll displays every morning and afternoon to ensure shelves are fully stocked without out-of-stock gaps.
- [ ] Perform daily morning dairy case inspections to verify expiration dates and rotate inventory across all fluid milk and cheese products.
- [ ] Brief cashiers on manually applying the $3 discount on qualifying baskets of $20 or more when customers show the campaign email or SMS.

### Measure
- Weekly spend trend among the 272 targeted Core customers relative to their $9.65 weekly spend baseline.
- Total redemption count of the $3 off $20 basket offer at the checkout register.
- Weekly revenue changes in the fluid milk products, cheese, and packaged bread categories.
- Average basket size of targeted Core customers during the 14-day campaign window.

### Risks
If shelf replenishment or dairy freshness inspections are not executed consistently prior to launch, returning regulars may encounter the same out-of-stock or dating issues and abandon the store permanently. Additionally, offering discounts without enforcing the $20 minimum basket could erode margins on customers already spending below the threshold.

## Core · Visiting less

**Goal:** Re-engage 163 drifting core regular customers and protect $2,700.75 in weekly revenue by addressing price sensitivity, staple stockouts, product freshness, and checkout speed.

### Offer: $3 Off Your Next $20 Basket of Fresh Staples
Suggested values for testing: Save $3 on any grocery purchase of $20 or more. Valid on in-store purchases of fresh dairy, packaged bread, deli, and soup aisle items when presented at checkout.

*Perk:* none  
*Economics:* $3 off $20 (15% of basket); median weekly spend $12.80; threshold above median ✅; economics ✅

*Why this offer:* Core customers in this segment average $12.80 weekly spend; establishing a $20 minimum basket threshold encourages basket expansion while the modest $3 discount (15% of $20) protects thin independent grocery margins. This addresses price perception issues (R5, R9) directly across cutback staple categories like milk, cheese, and packaged bread. (T6, T7, R5, R9)

### Before you send (owner checklist)
- [ ] Audit fluid milk and packaged bread stock daily at 7 AM to replenish shelves and eliminate stockouts.
- [ ] Inspect sell-by and expiration dates across the dairy and cheese displays every morning, immediately rotating stock and removing items nearing expiration.
- [ ] Assign secondary cashier backup at registers during peak rush hours from 4 PM to 7 PM to eliminate checkout bottlenecks.
- [ ] Brief register staff on the manual $3 discount redemption process for baskets of $20 or more.
- [ ] Verify explicit SMS consent records prior to deploying text message broadcasts.

### Measure

- Average weekly spend change compared to the segment baseline of $12.80.
- Category sales recovery within fluid milk, packaged bread, and cheese.
- Net weekly store revenue recovered against the $2,700.75 revenue at risk.

### Risks
Margin erosion if basket sizes do not expand beyond the $20 threshold, potential register bottlenecks if peak traffic surges occur before backup staff transition, and customer dissatisfaction if advertised dairy or bread inventory runs out.

## High-value · at risk

**Goal:** Re-engage 34 high-value at-risk shoppers and recover up to $2,579.53 in weekly revenue by addressing stockouts, milk freshness, and packaged bread assortment while providing dedicated personal recognition.

### Offer: A personal staple hold service plus $10 off your $75 basket as our valued shopper
These suggested values provide our most valued shoppers with an item-reservation service for daily essentials (fluid milk, packaged bread, and soups) plus $10 off any purchase of $75 or more. Values are suggestions and can be tailored to owner margins.

*Perk:* Personal staple reservation service to hold fresh milk, favorite packaged bread, and soups at the front counter upon request  
*Economics:* $10 off $75 (13% of basket); median weekly spend $70.93; threshold above median ✅; economics ✅

*Why this offer:* This high-value segment has a median weekly spend of $70.93 and cut back on milk, soup, and bread due to stockouts and freshness concerns. Setting the basket threshold at $75 protects margins while growing basket size, paired with recognition-focused personalized service rather than steep margin erosion. (T5, T7, R1, R2, R3)
